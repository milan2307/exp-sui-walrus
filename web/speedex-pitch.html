The Solution
One timestamp. On the blockchain. Both parties read it.
TradeProof records the gate-in moment on Sui — a public, immutable, neutral ledger.
  1. 1
    Shipping line registers the container on Sui A Container Digital ID is created on-chain. The ISO ID (e.g. MSCU9876543) is the object. The timestamp is set by the Sui network — not by any party.
  2. 2
    Gate-in is recorded on-chain When the container enters the terminal, gate_in() is called. The Sui blockchain records the exact millisecond. This cannot be changed by anyone — not the shipping line, not TradeProof, not Speedex.
  3. 3
    Demurrage is calculated from the on-chain timestamp Both parties use the same public formula on the same timestamp. They get the same number. There is nothing to dispute.
demurrage = (days_at_port − free_days) × rate_per_day
// Both parties run this. Same inputs. Same output.
No email. No credit note. No dispute.

🔐 Seal — Document Encryption (Mysten Labs, Sui Testnet) Testnet · Active
BL documents encrypted client-side with threshold encryption. Only shipper + consignee + bank hold key shares. The on-chain record carries only the SHA-256 hash. The Seal service checks the AllowList on-chain before releasing key shares.

TradeProof: competitor reads "encrypted blob exists" — nothing else
🛡️ Confidential Transactions — Private Settlement (Sui Testnet) Testnet · Active
Demurrage settled on-chain using homomorphic encryption. Both parties verify the settlement is correct and complete without revealing the amount to anyone else. Your negotiated rates stay private.
Web2: email invoices reveal your rates to forwarded recipients
TradeProof: on-chain shows "settled" — amount visible only to counterparties

Why Other Attempts Failed
TradeLens. we.trade. Contour. Marco Polo. All failed.
We studied every post-mortem. Three root causes. TradeProof fixes all three.
Three root causes
  • No privacy — competitors shared a ledger. No one joined.
  • Platform control — one company (Maersk, R3, ICC) controlled the network. Rivals refused.
  • Cold start — needed 100 parties to have value. Zero parties joined until there was value. Impossible loop.

TradeLens (IBM + Maersk): shut 2022 · Contour (banks): shut 2023 · we.trade: shut 2023 · Marco Polo: shut 2023

TradeProof — three fixes
  • Privacy first — Seal encryption means no party reads another's data. Competitors can coexist.
  • Protocol, not platform — open source Move contracts. Sui enforces rules, not TradeProof. No company controls the network.
  • Smallest viable pilot — two parties, one problem (demurrage), no consortium needed. Value from day 1.

The lesson: Privacy + no single controller + smallest viable start. That's the formula. That's TradeProof.
