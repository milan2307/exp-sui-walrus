How demurrage works on TradeProof
The gate-in timestamp is written to the Sui blockchain by the port authority when the container physically enters the terminal. It is immutable — nobody can change it after the fact. Both you and the shipping line calculate demurrage from this exact same timestamp using the same formula. There is no dispute possible over the number. The only questions are: what are the free days, and what is the agreed rate? Both of those are also on-chain.

How Demurrage Escrow Works — payment_escrow.move
🔒
1. FUND ESCROW
You deposit max demurrage (e.g. 30 days × $150 = $4,500 in SUI) into the smart contract. Neither party controls these funds — only the contract can release them.
⏱
2. CLOCK RUNS
Demurrage accrues from the on-chain gate-in timestamp. Both parties read the same immutable blockchain record — the same number, always. No dispute possible over the clock.
🚪
3. GATE-OUT
Port authority calls container::gate_out() on-chain. The exact gate-out timestamp is recorded by Sui validators.
✅
4. AUTO-SETTLE
Smart contract calculates exact owed amount. Releases it to the shipping line. Returns remainder to you. Zero human intervention required.

What is a Shipment?
A shipment is the end-to-end record linking a Bill of Lading, container, all documents, and payment events into one auditable chain. Every event — gate-in, vessel load, discharge, customs clearance — is timestamped by the Sui blockchain. Neither party can alter it after the fact.
