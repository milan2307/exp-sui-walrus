Privacy Architecture

Privacy was why TradeLens failed.
It's our first priority.

TradeProof uses three separate Sui privacy technologies — each solving a distinct problem that ended every previous trade finance blockchain.

🔐
Mysten Labs · Sui Testnet
Seal — Document Encryption
Full BL documents are encrypted client-side with Sui's Seal threshold encryption before upload to Walrus. Only authorized parties (shipper, consignee, bank) hold key shares. The on-chain record stores only the hash.
  • Competitor reads: "encrypted blob exists" — nothing more
  • BL number, cargo details, and trade routes stay private
  • Access managed on-chain via AllowList object
  • Bank added to AllowList when LC is presented; removed on surrender
Sui Testnet · Active
🛡️
Sui Protocol · Testnet
Confidential Transactions — Private Settlement
Demurrage and settlement payments use Sui's Confidential Transactions — homomorphic encryption hides the amount. Both parties verify the settlement is correct without revealing the number to anyone else.
  • Competitors see: "demurrage settled" — not the amount
  • Your negotiated rates with shipping lines stay private
  • Homomorphic encryption: math works on ciphertext
  • Settlement is final and verifiable without disclosure
Sui Testnet · Active
🪪
Sui Protocol · Mainnet
zkLogin — Zero-friction Onboarding
Any party — shipper, consignee, clearing agent, bank — can join with a Google account. Zero-knowledge proof maps their OAuth identity to a Sui address. No seed phrase, no wallet app, no blockchain expertise required.
  • Login with Google, Facebook, or Microsoft
  • Your Google identity is never stored on-chain
  • ZK proof: verifier knows you own the account, not which account
  • Nairobi clearing agent onboards in 30 seconds
